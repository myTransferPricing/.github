# Transfer pricing reference in plain markdown

We publish transfer pricing rules for 27 countries as plain markdown, adapted from the OECD transfer pricing country profiles, together with prompts for AI assistants. Everything is free to reuse under CC BY 4.0. It is maintained by the team behind [myTransferPricing](https://mytransferpricing.com), an AI-first transfer pricing platform for in-house tax teams and advisory firms, hosted in the EU.

## Why

Transfer pricing rules are public, but scattered across PDFs and hard to reuse. We rebuild them from the primary OECD sources as plain markdown, with the source and its date stated in every file, so a person can read them and an AI agent can load them into a context window or retrieval index without conversion. Every change is versioned in git.

## Published now

Everything lives in [transfer-pricing-reference](https://github.com/myTransferPricing/transfer-pricing-reference).
